# VibePM Product Plan

## Product promise

VibePM is a fast, local-first macOS project manager for individuals who want to capture work, organize it into Projects, and decide what to do Today without operating a team workspace.

## Primary workflow

1. Capture a Task into the Inbox in seconds.
2. Clarify its title, priority, schedule, and Project.
3. Work from Today, a Project list, Board, or Gantt view.
4. Complete the Task and retain its history.
5. Recover accidental deletions from Trash or an automatic Recovery Point.
6. Archive a Project when its outcome is complete or no longer active.

## MVP scope

- Projects: create, rename, archive, restore, inspect, move to Trash, recover, permanently delete individually, or empty Trash after confirmation.
- Tasks: create, edit, complete, prioritize, schedule, move between Projects, and move to Trash individually or in batches.
- Subtasks: one level of parent-child organization.
- Views: Inbox, Today, Projects, and per-Project List, Board, and Gantt planning with shared filtering and sorting.
- Productivity: keyboard-first quick capture, search, and native notifications.
- Ownership: local persistence, automatic Recovery Points, exact JSON restore, plus global and Project-scoped editable Excel exchange with preview and downloadable localized templates.
- Quality: dark mode, accessibility labels, immediate undo for destructive actions, surfaced save errors, and deterministic data migrations.

## Explicitly deferred

- Accounts, team workspaces, comments, and permissions.
- Real-time collaboration and a hosted backend.
- Resource capacity planning, billing, and time tracking.
- AI agents that modify project data without a review step.
- Windows, web, and mobile clients.

## Success criteria

- A new Task can be captured from anywhere in the app in under five seconds.
- The app launches into useful content without a network connection.
- No normal edit can silently delete a Project or Task.
- A user can export all owned data in a documented format.
